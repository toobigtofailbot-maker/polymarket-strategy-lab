## Corrected Hermes Philosophy

Hermes should be powerful from the beginning as an adaptive research brain.
Guardrails prevent execution and control risk; guardrails must not reduce
legitimate research usefulness. The repository should be built around structured Hermes
inputs and outputs for maximum later value without redesign, while all authority
to execute, validate, promote, or mutate runtime state remains outside Hermes.

Research breadth is intentionally broad. Safety is achieved by authority
separation, deterministic ownership of control surfaces, human gates, and
explicit future WPs, not by making Hermes a passive summarizer.

## Broad Research-Freedom Policy

Hermes research breadth should not be artificially minimized. A useful research
brain needs enough freedom to explore hypotheses, contradictions, ambiguous
rules, weak evidence, performance failures, wallet behavior, and market regimes.

The safety boundary is not "make Hermes weak." The safety boundary is:

- Hermes can research, critique, explain, and propose future review work.
- Deterministic systems and humans own execution, validation, risk, state,
  accounting, credentials, order lifecycle, live safety, kill switch, audit logs,
  and strategy-promotion gates.
- Any accepted Hermes observation must flow through human review, repo-truth
  updates, approved config changes, or a later approved WP before it can change
  behavior.

## Deterministic-Core Responsibilities

The deterministic core controls:

- execution;
- validation;
- risk;
- state;
- accounting;
- credentials;
- order lifecycle;
- live safety;
- kill switch;
- audit logs;
- strategy-promotion gates.

Hermes output cannot override, bypass, approve, or replace these controls.

## Forbidden Hermes Authority

Hermes must not:

- access credentials or private endpoints;
- place, cancel, sign, or submit orders;
- control runtime, scheduler, or bot loop;
- mutate live configs;
- override risk;
- approve strategy promotion;
- approve PRs or claim merge readiness;
- execute paper or live orders;
- act as the final decision-maker for trade, risk, or live actions.
