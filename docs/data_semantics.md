# Data Semantics

## Modes

- Historical: persisted past data used for research, backtests, audit, and reports.
- Live-read-only: current data observed without order placement authority.
- Paper: simulated orders and fills with no live venue effects.
- Live: real venue interaction; disabled until explicitly authorized.

## Terms

- Market: a tradable prediction market with one or more outcomes.
- Event: the real-world event or grouping that one or more markets reference.
- Outcome: a possible result within a market.
- Token/asset_id: venue-specific tradable identifier for an outcome.
- Order book snapshot: point-in-time bid/ask depth for a market or token.
- Trade: an exchange-reported transaction or internal simulated transaction.
- Fill: execution against an order, real or simulated.
- Order: instruction to buy or sell if later authorized.
- Active order: order currently open at the venue or in a simulator.
- Canceled order: order canceled before full execution.
- Failed order: order that could not be accepted, canceled, or reconciled as expected.
- Signal: non-binding analytical observation.
- TradeProposal: strategy output requesting risk review; not an order.
- RiskDecision: deterministic approval, rejection, sizing, or disablement result.
- Paper trade: simulated trade in paper mode.
- Live trade: real trade in live mode after explicit authorization.
- Simulated fill: paper-mode fill generated by a simulator.
- Realized PnL: closed profit or loss.
- Unrealized PnL: mark-to-market profit or loss on open exposure.
- Wallet: public blockchain address or venue account identifier when permitted.
- Watched wallet: wallet selected for observation.
- Copied trade: paper or future authorized live action derived from a watched wallet event.
- Missed copy trade: eligible wallet event not copied due to delay, liquidity, limits, or controls.
- Stale data: data older than the maximum allowed age for a decision path.
