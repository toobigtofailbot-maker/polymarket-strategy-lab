## Self-Diagnosis

- Follow-up required: YES. A same-PR contract correction fixed three
  contract-level blockers on the first reviewed head
  (`0d13c1baeba4750c00940b2e82ac2d53c712bd38`) before merge.
- Preventable: YES — all three were preventable at the architecture /
  draftability gate.
- Blocker A (identity/back-link contradiction): the first draft allowed
  `created_from_manifest_entry_id` to be null before registration and non-null
  after, letting one `dataset_snapshot_reference_id` identify two contents.
  Corrected: the field is reserved and fixed null in schema `1.0.0`, provenance
  is one-way, a non-null backlink rejects with `FIELD_VALUE_INVALID`, and an
  explicit immutability invariant forbids any excluded field changing under the
  same ID. Preventable by threat-modeling identity stability against every
  excluded field at draft time.
- Blocker B (SUPERSEDE/INVALIDATE ambiguity): the first draft did not state
  whether a transition entry's `dataset_snapshot_reference_id` was the target or a
  replacement. Corrected: SUPERSEDE/INVALIDATE are state-transition records whose
  `dataset_snapshot_reference_id` MUST equal the target entry's; replacements are
  registered separately; state-transition integrity (no repeated terminal
  transition, no cross-manifest/future/self/mismatched target) is defined; the
  chain head is separated from subject-reference state. Preventable by writing the
  exact subject/target invariant before locking the vocabulary.
- Blocker C (examples were prose): the first draft's "examples" were numbered
  descriptions. Corrected: twelve complete machine-readable JSON wrapper objects
  parsed by the focused test with `json.loads` and checked for structure and
  invariants, not phrase presence. Preventable by requiring concrete parseable
  objects at the draftability gate.
- Architecture gate completeness: contract-only decisions complete after the
  correction; no runtime, storage, or dependency decision is taken.
- Scope-guard maintenance: successor exact-scope guards updated with justification
  and without weakening; the correction stayed within the same 11-file scope
  (canonical doc and focused test edited in place).
- Evidence corrections before publication: this fragment, project status, repo
  truth, and the PR body were updated to stop claiming the examples were complete
  before concrete objects existed, that no architecture ambiguity remained, and
  that no follow-up was required on the first reviewed head.
