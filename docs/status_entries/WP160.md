## Implemented surface

- New import-safe, side-effect-free, standard-library-only module
  `manifest_reference.py` implementing:
  - immutable frozen dataclasses `DatasetSnapshotReference`, `ManifestEntry`,
    `DatasetSnapshotReferenceBuildResult`, `ManifestEntryBuildResult`, and
    `ManifestChainValidationResult`;
  - `build_dataset_snapshot_reference`, `build_manifest_entry`, and
    `validate_manifest_chain`;
  - the locked constants `MANIFEST_REFERENCE_LABEL`,
    `MANIFEST_REFERENCE_POSTURE`, the schema id/version pairs, the closed
    vocabularies (`DATASET_DERIVATION_MODES`, `MANIFEST_ENTRY_KINDS`,
    `REFERENCE_STATES`, `MANIFEST_REASON_CODES`),
    `MANIFEST_REFERENCE_VALIDATION_STATUSES`, and
    `MANIFEST_REFERENCE_ERROR_CODES`.
- Deterministic identity derivation matching the WP158 contract preimages
  (`datasetref:sha256:` and `manifestentry:sha256:`), recompute-and-compare
  integrity, and bounded fail-closed results with an always-empty
  `warning_codes` tuple.
- Builders validate one object's local structure only; `validate_manifest_chain`
  re-derives and revalidates every entry and reference, enforces the
  single-manifest hash-linked append chain, resolves subjects against a supplied
  registry, applies the register/supersede/invalidate state machine, and binds
  human-review posture.
- Package `__init__.py` re-exports the twenty approved public symbols; the
  WP156/WP157 public surface is unchanged.
- A clearly marked WP160 implementation addendum (Section 19) in the canonical
  contract document, with the existing twelve contract examples preserved.
