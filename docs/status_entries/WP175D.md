## Capability and evidence

WP175D adds one capability: validated PMXT v2 external-history events can become a
content-addressed, provenance-separated WP170 dataset binding and standard
`ResearchDatasetManifest` while retaining real source availability time and
without acquiring canonical-PIT authority. The additive surfaces are a
`PmxtV2ResearchLineage` over explicitly selected existing WP168 ordered event
digests (shared canonical row-order/digest implementation with
`convert_pmxt_v2_events`), an `ExternalDatasetBinding` subtype (source_kind
`WP167_EXTERNAL_DATASET_DESCRIPTOR`, generic `datasetbinding:sha256:` identity, no
`DatasetSnapshotReference`, no `SOURCE_KIND_CANONICAL_PIT`), and a narrow PMXT ->
WP170 bridge module. Member `availability_time_ns` is exactly PMXT
`timestamp_received` / `ts_init`; `event_time_ns` is source timestamp / `ts_event`
and is diagnostic only. External samples cite only `externalmember:sha256:` ids
bound to the exact lineage and are admitted only when
`feature_availability_time` is at or after the maximum cited member availability
and at or before `decision_time`.

## Registry and predecessor compatibility

`ExternalDatasetBinding` persists under the existing `DATASET_BINDING` record kind
with exact `schema_id` subtype dispatch; `REGISTRY_FORMAT_ID`,
`REGISTRY_SCHEMA_VERSION`, `REGISTRY_APPLICATION_ID`, the SQLite
table/trigger/index schema, and the `DATASET_BINDING` CHECK vocabulary are
unchanged. Old canonical-only registries remain readable with no migration,
repair, or rewrite. `ConvertedEventStream` public fields/identity, canonical
`DatasetBinding` fields and `build_dataset_binding`, and
`build_preregistered_experiment` public signature are unchanged;
preregistration now admits either binding subtype without changing OOS,
first-look sealing, or evidence lifecycle rules.
