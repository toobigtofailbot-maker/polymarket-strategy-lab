## Meaning Of Deferral

There is no active candidate, shortlist, selection, scheduled research,
provider implementation, remote experiment, account, credential, endpoint,
model-call, spend, or persistence work. The Hermes research architecture and
local deterministic workflow remain intact. Deferral does not prove
impossibility, model quality, paper readiness, or local-model feasibility.

## Revisit Triggers

Allowed trigger classes are current directly relevant first-party public
evidence, sanitized account-specific operator evidence, current sanitized
contractual evidence, and an official architecture change plausibly satisfying
unchanged mandatory requirements. Actual operator hardware evidence may gate a
separate local feasibility decision but does not reopen the remote track.

Every trigger requires a concrete bounded artifact that could change one named
mandatory row from a non-passing state to `PASS`.

## Non-Triggers

Elapsed time, a model release without relevant control evidence, price,
benchmarks, social media, marketing, repeated help or Tier 2 content, aliases,
unsupported sales/support claims, unsanitized login-only controls, unchanged
documentation, sessions, WP numbers, impatience, forced-shortlist preference,
local-harness completeness, persistent zero readiness, industry adoption,
another provider's posture, cost compatibility, and Hermes compatibility do
not trigger a revisit.

## Materiality Standard

A trigger is material only when it could change one exact mandatory gate to
`PASS` for the same candidate/API/account/credential/contract/deployment
posture. It records trigger identity/type, exact posture and gate, prior state,
new evidence and type, evidence date, freshness, materiality explanation,
remaining blockers, and sanitization.

Public evidence is current and accessed within 30 days; undated pages are
re-accessed within 7 days. Operator controls are captured within 30 days and
rate/spend limits within 7 days. Contractual evidence is effective and
reviewed within 30 days. Architecture/hardware evidence is current and
captured/accessed within 30 days. Superseded evidence is stale. Freshness alone
is never materiality.

## Revisit Evidence Packet

The packet identifies the exact candidate/provider/API/model/architecture and
mandatory gate; prior state; new source or sanitized operator evidence; source
or effective date; access date; source/evidence class; direct/inferred class;
account/contract dependency; eligibility effect; remaining blockers; and
no-secret/no-personal-data confirmation.
