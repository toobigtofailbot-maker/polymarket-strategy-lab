## 10.1 Stable host identity

Derive a stable pseudonymous `host_instance_id` from the host's local `/etc/machine-id` bytes without ever emitting the raw value.

```text
host_instance_id =
  "pmhost-v1:" + sha256(
      b"polymarket-execution-host-v1\0" + normalized_machine_id_bytes
```

- require canonical UUID text or equivalently strict validated UUID input;
