Implement src/research_runtime/executable_edge as a local/offline research boundary.

ResearchDecisionTrace binds experiment_id and authoritative first-look OOS evidence, carries deterministic ordered market/asset decision events with decision timestamp, research side/action, intended quantity, and pre-decision value/reference inputs, contains no account/order identifiers, and has a content-addressed identity.

ExecutionCostModel is content-addressed and versioned. It explicitly records aggressive/taker or passive/maker mode, fee schedule/reference, latency, TTL, size/capacity grid, passive queue-ahead assumption, adverse-selection markout horizon, continuity/staleness bound, fill/capacity thresholds, and frozen net-edge threshold. Unknown fee values never default to favorable zero; maker rebate defaults to explicit zero absent validated local evidence.

The deterministic evaluator is event-time/PIT only. Aggressive evaluation sweeps visible book depth at simulated arrival and preserves partial/unfilled quantities. Passive evaluation requires subsequent eligible trade flow to consume visible queue ahead; touch alone cannot fill. Latency shifts arrival. Stale/gapped/unavailable truth closes inconclusive. Fees, spread, slippage, latency/markout, fill, and capacity use exact decimal/integer arithmetic. Default Nautilus fills cannot prove executable edge.

ExecutableEdgeAssessment references exact WP170 experiment and first-look evidence, decision trace, cost model, and WP168 market-data lineage. It reports gross research result, component costs, fill ratio, net result, capacity result, assumptions, and deterministic identity. Closed statuses are EXECUTABLE_EDGE_PASS, EXECUTABLE_EDGE_FAIL, EXECUTABLE_EDGE_INCONCLUSIVE, and DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY. wp173_evaluation_eligible is true only for authoritative first-look evidence, exact cost-model binding, no reused evidence, complete/continuous data, passing fill/capacity thresholds, and passing frozen net-edge threshold. Null/mismatched cost references are DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY.

Persistence uses deterministic serialization/validation and explicit caller-supplied local output. Existing different bytes are never overwritten; identical bytes are idempotent. Generated assessments are not committed. Do not add a strategy generator, optimizer, search, portfolio engine, or paper engine.
